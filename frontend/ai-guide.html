Purpose and Intended Use

MeDIAuto AI supports viewing pathology slides, annotating tissue and cells, and reviewing AI analysis. Quanti HE provides H&E detection and analysis; Quanti IHC analyzes selected immunohistochemistry markers; VS IHC generates virtual staining images.

Select the model, tissue type, and marker appropriate for the project and slide. Review outputs alongside the original image. Do not base diagnosis or treatment decisions solely on AI output.

Input Requirements

Inputs include pathology images and your selections of model, tissue type, marker, and region of interest. A file that can be opened is not necessarily suitable for a particular AI model. Match H&E tissue settings and IHC marker settings to the actual slide.

Registered extensions include SVS, NDPI, VMS, VMU, SCN, MRXS, TIFF/TIF, PNG/JPG/JPEG, iSyntax/i2syntax, and ZIP. Opening some formats requires the appropriate reader, SDK, and companion files. ZIP is an upload package, not an image analyzed directly by AI. Registration does not establish validation across all models, scanners, and resolutions. VS IHC defaults to a target resolution of 2.0 µm/px. Other models also use microns per pixel (MPP); check the source metadata.

View model-specific training dataset records — sources, collection counts, and preprocessing from F02-1.

Limitations

Blur, tissue folds, staining and scanner differences, incorrect pixel-size metadata, and region selection can affect results. Unsupported or damaged files and missing model weights can cause processing to fail.

Check image quality and match model settings to the actual stain. Results obtained with different models, weights, or preprocessing are not directly equivalent. Do not use failed or incomplete processing as a completed analysis.

Understanding Results

Depending on the model, outputs include detection locations, cell classes, counts, scores, or virtual staining images. A displayed count represents model detections; it does not guarantee the true total cell count or a correct diagnosis.

Confidence is a model output, not a calibrated probability of a correct diagnosis or a measure of clinical accuracy. Check the analyzed region when interpreting results. Virtual stains are AI-generated images and must not be treated as equivalent to actual immunohistochemistry test results.

Risks and Review

Missed detections, false detections, classification errors, and inaccurate generated details may lead to incorrect interpretation. Overreliance on AI may bypass necessary review.

Compare outputs with the source slide. Pause use and obtain review when locations, classes, or scores appear inconsistent. Intended use requires appropriate validation and a review process because incorrect outputs may affect subsequent decisions.

Quantitative VS-IHC evaluation and the mapping of some evaluated components to deployed weights require further confirmation. Project completion, training curves, and visual examples do not establish clinical performance for this installation.
