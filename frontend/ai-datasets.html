Training Dataset Records

Source: F02-1 Data Collection, Labeling and Preprocessing Register, revision 01 (September 18, 2026). Counts below describe collection and dataset construction as recorded in the register; they are not evaluation-set sizes or training/validation split counts.

Quanti HE · Breast

AI-Hub 71831 / 20,000 PNG images, 1024×1024. Normal tissue: 6,500; in situ carcinoma: 6,500; invasive carcinoma: 7,000. Training input: 512×512 with cropping/padding and label-coordinate conversion.

Quanti HE · Stomach

AI-Hub 71830 / 10,000 PNG images, 1024×1024. Gastritis, intestinal-type, diffuse-type, and mixed-type groups: 2,500 each. Training input: 512×512. This record does not establish a separate dataset count for HE Other.

VS IHC

CODiPAI / 505 cases, 2,525 WSI. H&E, HER2, ER, PR, and Ki-67: 505 slides each; 505 case pairs per marker. Serial sections are not pixel-aligned ground-truth pairs. Final input: 512×512.

Quanti IHC · HER2

CODiPAI / 505 HER2 WSI. The register records labeling by three specialists. Preprocessing: 0.5 µm/px, 512×512; patches with fewer than 10 annotated cells are excluded.

Quanti IHC · ER/PR

CODiPAI / 505 ER + 505 PR = 1,010 WSI. The register records labeling by three specialists and DAB/overlay quality checks. Preprocessing: 0.5 µm/px, 512×512.

Quanti IHC · Ki-67

CODiPAI / 505 Ki-67 WSI. ER/PR nuclear annotations and a shared detector are also used. Preprocessing: 0.5 µm/px, 512×512. Positive/negative interpretation and the Ki-67 Index are handled separately.

This page summarizes the register; it is not a new inspection of the original training data. Consult the model-specific records for augmentation and cleaning details. Do not assume that every ER/PR augmentation setting or independently measured performance applies identically to Ki-67.
